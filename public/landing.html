Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, Google API setup, image fidelity, and commercial rights.

How does the lifetime plan and monthly allowance work?

Your software license is a permanent one-time purchase with no monthly AIProdGen platform subscription fees. Your monthly generation limit (e.g., 500 for Hobbyist, 3,000 for Pro, 25,000 for Agency) automatically resets on the 1st of every month for life. Image generation uses your own Google Gemini API key (BYOK).

What is BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) and what does Google charge?

BYOK means you connect your own free Google Gemini API key. Instead of paying us marked-up subscription rates, you pay Google directly at their raw wholesale price (~$0.039 per image generation). A batch of 100 studio images costs under $4.00 directly on Google Cloud. Generating marketing copy and product descriptions is pennies or free under Google's tier.

Do I retain full commercial rights to my generated assets?

Yes. All paid plans (Hobbyist, Pro Founder, and Agency) grant you full commercial rights to use your generated imagery and copywriting across your online store, marketplace listings, social media, and paid advertising. The Agency plan additionally includes commercial client-deliverable rights so you can sell deliverables to third-party clients.

How does AIProdGen preserve my product's logos, text, and shape?

Your uploaded photo is used as a strict geometric reference. AI diffusion maintains the silhouette, materials, and colors of your item while generating clean studio lighting or lifestyle environments. As with all generative AI, micro-typography, legal nutrition labels, and barcodes should always be visually inspected before final commercial printing or listing.

Are outputs compliant with Amazon and Shopify requirements?

Yes. The Commercial Photography engine specifically generates pure white RGB (255, 255, 255) backgrounds with clean margins, meeting Amazon's main hero image standards and Shopify catalog specifications.

How do 4K upscale credits work?

Pro Founder includes 50 starter 4K upscale credits, and Agency includes 120 starter credits. These credits do not expire and allow you to upscale images to ultra-high-resolution 4K for print packaging or high-DPI displays. If you need more upscaling credits in the future, affordable top-up packages are available directly in your dashboard.

Can I test the product before buying a lifetime plan?

Absolutely. You can sign up for the Free Explorer plan with 20 free monthly generations to test the studio, upload photos, and see the output quality firsthand. When you are ready for commercial watermark removal and higher allowances, you can upgrade to a lifetime plan with one click.
