Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about image fidelity, licensing, and marketplace rules.

Do I retain full commercial rights to my generated assets?

Yes. On all paid plans, you receive a full, royalty-free commercial license to use all generated imagery, ad packs, and marketing copy for your Shopify store, Amazon listings, physical packaging, social advertisements, and client campaigns.

Will my product's logos, text, and physical shape stay intact?

Yes. Unlike generic image generators that redraw your product from text, AIProdGen isolates your actual physical product and locks its geometry, packaging text, and brand colors. Only the surrounding environment, lighting reflections, and contact shadows are rendered.

Are the outputs compliant with Amazon's main image requirements?

Absolutely. The Commerce Engine produces pure white backgrounds (#FFFFFF) formatted to Amazon's exact 85%+ product coverage standards and high zoom resolutions (2000px+), ensuring zero rejections from Seller Central.

How does the lifetime plan work?

You pay once and never receive a monthly bill. You receive access to all current and upcoming engines, including your plan's included generations and upscaling capabilities.
