Pricing FAQ

What does "Lifetime" mean?

Pay once, use forever. No recurring charges. You keep your plan forever, including all future updates and features.

What is BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)?

You use your own Google Gemini API key. Google offers a free tier, so you can generate images at no additional cost beyond your LTD purchase.

Do I need my own API key?

Yes, all LTD plans use BYOK (Bring Your Own Key). Your own free Google Gemini API key powers all generations — no markup, no limits from us.

What's a "generation"?

Each time the AI creates a product photo counts as one generation. All 6 variations from a single prompt count as 1 generation.
